StarCare: Hospital Management System

A role-based hospital web app where patients book appointments, doctors manage their schedules, and admins run the hospital.

  • Three roles, three dashboards: admin, doctor and patient, each with its own login and permissions.
  • Smart booking: time-slot selection that blocks double-booking and caps a doctor's appointments per day.
  • Admin workflows: doctor approval, department management, filtering and appointment history search.
  • Live updates with AJAX for approvals, status changes and counts, plus email notifications.
  • Deployed by me on Render with WhiteNoise and environment-based settings, with seeded demo data.
